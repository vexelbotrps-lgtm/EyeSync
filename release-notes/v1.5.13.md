# EyeSync 1.5.13

Fewer missed clicks.

- **The tick always comes 0.3 s into a blink.** Calibrating no longer moves it later. Calibration asks for slow, half-second blinks, so it put the tick at 0.35–0.45 s, later than many quick clicks. Those blinks opened just before the tick and were lost: they were the only meant clicks missing from a real user's log. Your saved calibration is used as it is; there's no need to calibrate again. The trade-off: in 250 minutes of public video of people reading aloud, blinks clicked by mistake 1.89 times a minute at 0.3 s, against 1.48 at 0.35 s. In the user's own recorded blink tests, no extra blinks clicked by mistake.
- **Every missed click is logged.** A blink of 0.3 s or more that didn't click is now always written to `eyesync.log` ("too short for a click" or "the eyes were opening before the tick"). Before, at most one was noted every 20 seconds.
