# EyeSync 1.5.12

Faster everywhere it was measurably possible without making EyeSync less steady.

- **Quicker clicks.** After calibrating, the tick now comes at most 0.35 s into a blink (1.5.10–1.5.11: up to 0.45 s). Your saved calibration is used as it is — no need to calibrate again. The trade-off: in 250 minutes of public video of people reading aloud, blinks clicked by mistake 1.48 times a minute at 0.35 s, against 0.73 at 0.45 s (1.89 at the default 0.3 s).
- **Quicker start after Connect.** About 1 second less: EyeSync no longer loads a plotting library (matplotlib) that the face tracker drags in but never uses — EyeSync.exe leaves it out entirely — and the face model is no longer loaded twice when the camera is ready first.
- **Snappier cursor.** The cursor now follows the average of the last two nose positions instead of the middle of the last three: about half a camera frame less delay, and steady turns move the cursor slightly more evenly than before. (Following only the newest position would cut more delay but made slow movements jerky, so it wasn't done.)
