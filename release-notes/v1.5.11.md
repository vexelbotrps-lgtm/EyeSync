# EyeSync 1.5.11

A fix for blink clicks while you turn your head.

- **Once you hear the tick, turning your head no longer cancels the click.** EyeSync ignores closures where the eyes only *look* closed because the head moved (for example to the bottom of the screen). Until 1.5.10 that check could still cancel a closure after its tick, when the head drifted a little during a long closure — the click you were promised didn't come. 1.5.10's log showed two such closures (1.1 s long, the head drifting 0.17–0.18 face widths against a limit of 0.15). Eyes that only look closed are still caught before the tick.
- **Better log lines for ignored closures.** They now say whether the head moved sideways or up-down, and a closure that began while you were turning is logged too (it used to be silent), so any remaining missed click can be traced.
