# EyeSync 1.5.10

Fixes for the calibrated blink tick that 1.5.9 introduced, tested on a real user's recorded blinks and on 250 minutes of public video (10 GRID readers).

- **Your calibration blinks now click.** 1.5.9 timed each slow calibration blink from "eyelids a quarter down" until fully open again and set the tick at 90% of that, but the tick only comes while the eyes are shut. So the tick could fall after your eyes had begun to open, and the very blink you showed it didn't click. 1.5.10 times only how long the blinks stayed shut.
- **The tick is at most about half a second** (1.5.9: up to 0.65 s). On the 10 public readers a 0.45 s tick still means 61% fewer false clicks than the default ⅓ s (0.73 instead of 1.89 a minute), without making every click slow.
- **The double tick stays a second after the tick.** With 1.5.9's later tick it stayed at 1.3 s, leaving too little time to open your eyes, so a left click could come out as a right click.
- **Calibrate:** the blink step now says "eyes closed for about half a second". Calibrate again after updating to fit the tick; until then it stays at ⅓ second. (1.5.9's saved timing isn't used.)
- The camera view's "keep closed / open: CLICK / RIGHT CLICK" banner follows the actual ticks.
- When a closure is ignored because the head moved and the eyes only looked half closed, the log now says how far the eyelids closed and how far the head moved.
