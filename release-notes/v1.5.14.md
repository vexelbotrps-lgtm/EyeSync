# EyeSync 1.5.14

No more surprise calibrate beeps.

- **The calibrate gesture needs a real pull.** "Mouth to your right, 3 s" now starts only when the face tracker sees the mouth itself move to the side. Before, a smile that rose more on one side could start it: it beeped and froze the cursor and clicks until the face relaxed, and a lopsided smile held for 3 seconds could even start a calibration. A real user's log had three such beeps in one morning, just before a smile pause or a head tilt. Every mouth pull in their recorded test still starts the gesture. In 250 minutes of public video of people reading aloud, it never set the gesture off, before or after.
- **A clearer log.** If the calibrate warning ever sounds, `eyesync.log` now records what the mouth was doing at that moment.
